About Val

A creator journey that started with a dream.

I've wanted to become a content creator since I was nine years old. At 14, I started turning that dream into something real.

I originally started TikTok with friends, hoping we'd all become influencers together. Life changed, but I kept creating and continued building my own identity as Val.

Growing up, I loved anime, horses, dirt bikes, and other interests that didn't always feel shared by the people around me. Finding people online with similar interests made me feel seen. That's the kind of space I want to create for other people.

I'm quite nice, relatable, funny, and deeply interested in anime. I want people who find my page to feel welcome instead of left out.
